## How I will judge each citation

- **Supports:** the opened official source contains the same figure, period, unit and meaning.
- **Partly supports:** it confirms only part of the statement or uses a different period/definition.
- **Does not support:** it is unrelated, secondary, inaccessible, or does not contain the claimed figure.

A search snippet or an AI summary is not enough. Open the source itself and record the exact table, month and unit.
