# Step 7: Corrected brief and "What Not to Do" guide

## Part A — Corrected investor brief

### RupeeRise: India's digital lending and UPI environment

RupeeRise is a fictional Bengaluru-based digital lending app. India's digital payments infrastructure provides important context for digital financial services, but national UPI activity should not be confused with demand for RupeeRise's loans or with the company's own performance.

NPCI reports that UPI processed **25,466.70 million transactions**, with a total value of **₹31,52,929.46 crore**, in **October 2026**. These are official monthly payment-system figures, not RupeeRise customer or lending figures. [NPCI UPI Product Statistics](https://www.npci.org.in/product/upi/product-statistics)

For digital lending, the relevant RBI framework includes the **Reserve Bank of India (Digital Lending) Directions, 2025**, dated **8 May 2025**, reference **RBI/2025-26/36**. The Directions include requirements concerning regulated entities' arrangements with Lending Service Providers, borrower disclosures, loan disbursal and repayment, cooling-off periods, grievance redressal and data handling. [RBI Digital Lending Directions, 2025](https://www.rbi.org.in/scripts/BS_ViewMasDirections.aspx?id=12848)

The RBI Governor's official address at Digital Payments Awareness Week on **10 March 2025** discusses the convenience, safety and financial-inclusion potential of digital payments and UPI. No quotation about digital lending being "the bridge" to formal credit is used here because that wording could not be verified in the speech. [RBI Governor's speech](https://www.rbi.org.in/Scripts/BS_SpeechesView.aspx?Id=1496)

**Not verified:** the US$85 billion market-size claim, 28% forecast CAGR, 950 million active digital banking users, 41% growth in young salaried personal loans, and the claimed invented circular/framework references. These figures are excluded rather than repeated as facts.

RupeeRise must provide its own audited or officially released company metrics before making claims about users, disbursals, repayment performance or market share. This brief is a verified-context draft, not a substitute for legal or investment review.

## Part B — What not to do: a guide for safe AI use in finance

| Rule | Evidence from my log |
|---|---|
| 1. Never forward an AI-generated market number without checking the original official table. | C01: AI gave 31.8bn and ₹42.6 lakh crore; NPCI showed 25,466.70m and ₹31,52,929.46 crore. |
| 2. Do not use market-size estimates without a traceable official report and definition. | C02: US$85bn had no supporting RBI/PIB source in the checked set. |
| 3. Treat growth forecasts as forecasts, not established facts. | C03: 28% CAGR had no official source identified. |
| 4. Check what a user-count metric actually measures before presenting it. | C04: "active digital banking users" was not supported by a cited official definition or figure. |
| 5. Do not invent a customer segment growth statistic to make a pitch stronger. | C05: 41% young-salaried personal-loan growth was unsupported. |
| 6. Verify the exact circular number, date and title on the regulator's website. | C06: claimed reference/date differed from RBI's official 2025 Directions. |
| 7. Read the actual regulatory paragraph; do not paraphrase it into a new obligation. | C07: "own escrow account" misstated the fund-flow requirements. |
| 8. Search the regulator's official index before quoting a framework title. | C08: "UPI Lending Security Framework 2025" was not found in the checked RBI source set. |
| 9. Never turn a general security principle into a universal legal requirement. | C09: biometric checks before every repayment were not found in the Directions. |
| 10. Never publish a quotation without checking the original speech or transcript. | C10: the attributed quote and event were not present in the RBI speech checked. |

## My answer to the founder

No, the original AI brief should not be sent to investors as it is. Several figures and regulatory references were distorted, unsupported or fabricated, and the quotation could not be verified. Use only the corrected official-source facts, add RupeeRise's own substantiated company data, and obtain compliance review before circulation.
